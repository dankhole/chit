  • Less Is More

    Tot’s single window design and simple formatting controls mean no more hunting for that chunk of text.

  • iCloud Sync

    Seamlessly syncs your text across all devices using iCloud, so you can capture thoughts wherever you are.

  • Dark / Light Mode

    Redesigned for Liquid Glass, Tot looks stunning day and night and takes advantage of iOS & macOS themes.

  • Markdown Support

    Full support for rich or plain text and even automatically translates text to Markdown for handy use on the web.

  • Shortcuts Support

    Includes actions that let Tot store and retrieve text. A great scratchpad for your workflows on iOS and macOS!

  • Killer Keyboard

    Tot puts common text operations on the keyboard and keeps track of word and character counts as you type.

  • Customizable Smart Bullets

    Put a fun spin on your boring checklists and to-dos. Dividers are customizable, too!

  • Automatic Backups

    Tot regularly backs up your text automatically to keep it safe and sound.
